# SwiftPDF Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

SwiftPDF is made by yasmanyfmSoft. This policy explains how the SwiftPDF apps for Windows and Android handle your information.

## The short version

SwiftPDF does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Your documents never leave your device.

## Your documents

- PDFs you open, edit, fill or sign are processed entirely on your device.
- Files are only saved where you choose to save them (on Android, the `Documents/SwiftPDF` folder or the app you share to).
- SwiftPDF does not upload your documents to any server.

## Signatures

Signatures you choose to save are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. You can delete them at any time from the signature dialog, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

## Data collection

SwiftPDF has no user accounts and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services. It does not collect location, contacts, device identifiers or usage data.

## Permissions

- **Files / storage:** only to open the files you pick and save the files you export.
- **Internet:** included by the app framework; SwiftPDF itself does not send your data anywhere.

## Sharing

When you use the Share button, your PDF is passed to the app you choose (for example email or messaging). That app's own privacy policy then applies.

## Children

SwiftPDF is a general-purpose productivity app and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
